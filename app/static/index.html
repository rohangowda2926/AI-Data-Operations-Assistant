How the assistant works

The service is deliberately transparent. Every result can be traced back to a row in your upload and a documented rule.

01

Validate

Checks headers, dates, unique order IDs, quantities, prices, encoding, and size limits. Invalid files are rejected as a whole.

02

Calculate

Uses exact decimal arithmetic for revenue, average order value, units sold, daily sales, and product totals.

03

Review

Flags unusually large orders with a median and MAD rule, plus zero-revenue orders. Flags are prompts for human review.
